### 13. Misc one-off gaps (18, plus 9 flagged separately below as bugs)
- [`identifiers`](https://github.com/ballerina-platform/ballerina-distribution/tree/master/examples/identifiers) — `\u{H}` Unicode escape sequences inside identifiers aren't normalized during symbol resolution, so a name written two ways (plain vs. partially-escaped) resolves to two different symbols ("unused variable"/"Unknown symbol" for what is really one identifier).
